# Synthetic dataset — the scenario in plain words

Describes the **built-in synthetic scenario** — the 7 CSVs in `../data/synthetic_data/`,
written in the exact same schema as `../data/input_data/` so the two can be compared or
swapped 1:1. Generated by `../code/export_synthetic_data.jl` (re-run it to regenerate the
CSVs and this README).

Run it like the real data (from the `code/` folder):

```julia
run_scenario_1(mode = :input, input_dir = "../data/synthetic_data")
```

## What's in this scenario

- **1 mobile charging station(s) (MCS)** — drive around the site and charge
  the excavators. Battery 50.0–250.0 kWh (starts at 250.0), charges from the grid at up to 150.0 kW, with 2 plug(s) at up to 60.0 kW each.
- **2 construction electric vehicle(s) (CEVs / excavators)** — battery 18.0–90.0 kWh (starts at 72.0), receive up to 45.0 kW.
- **3 locations (nodes)**:
  - `i1` = the **grid connection** (where the MCS recharges). No excavator here.
  - `i2` = a **work site**, home of excavator `e1`.
  - `i3` = a **work site**, home of excavator `e2`.

## The work each site must get done (PER DAY)

Work is a **per-day schedule** (not one lumpsum): each reported day has its own quota.
For the exported CSV set the `place.csv` quota below is used unless a `work_by_day.csv`
is added; the built-in `:synthetic` mode ships a two-day schedule that differs by day.

| Site | Excavator | Digging (default/day 1) | Loading / swinging (default/day 1) |
|------|-----------|---------|--------------------|
| `i2` | `e1` | 2.5 h | 1.5 h |
| `i3` | `e2` | 1.5 h | 1.0 h |

## How far apart things are (MCS travel time, in 15-min steps)

| From \ To | `i1` | `i2` | `i3` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `i1` | – | 2.0 (30 min) | 3.0 (45 min) |
| `i2` | 2.0 (30 min) | – | 2.0 (30 min) |
| `i3` | 3.0 (45 min) | 2.0 (30 min) | – |

## The day (repeated across the multi-day horizon)

The controller runs a multi-day, cross-day receding horizon (`n_days` reported days
plus one dropped buffer day). Each day carries **its own** dig/load quota (from
`work_by_day.csv` when present, otherwise the `place.csv` default repeated), and the
CEV batteries plus any unfinished work carry over from one day to the next.

- **Daytime (8:00–18:00, 40 × 15-min steps):** the excavators dig / load / travel / idle; the
  MCS drives between the grid and the sites to keep them charged. The MCS must be parked
  at the grid by **each** 18:00. The excavators are **not** forced back to their start
  charge every evening — their battery flows across days; the "return to start"
  (energy-neutral) rule only applies at the very end of the horizon.
- **Overnight (every night):** a cheapest-hours refill tops the MCS back up, closing its
  nightly energy-neutral cycle so it starts each morning full.
- The daily dig/load quota is **cumulative**: a fresh day's hours arrive each morning and
  any shortfall rolls over into the next day.
- Electricity price and grid-carbon intensity vary across all 96 quarter-hours of
  the day (see `time_data.csv`); the same price/carbon profile is applied each day,
  while the **work quota can differ per day** (`work_by_day.csv`).

## Note on the numbers

The **activity power draws are treated as unknown** in the model — the controller learns
them online from telematics. The per-interval work caps in `work_flexible.csv` are a
large, non-binding value (they only mark *when* each excavator is allowed to work).
