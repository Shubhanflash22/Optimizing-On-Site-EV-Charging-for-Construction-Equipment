15:45 — MPC under-charges the CEV during the afternoon service stop
Both A0 and A1S arrive at the CEV station at 15:45 with nearly equal SOE (~7.6–7.7 kWh). A0's open-loop plan pre-committed a full 8.00 kW transfer (+2.00 kWh). A1S committed only 0.21 kW (+0.05 kWh). The two approaches exit the interval 1.887 kWh apart: A0 at 9.617 kWh, A1S at 7.730 kWh.
2
The 1.887 kWh gap persists unchanged through 16:00–16:30
No subsequent charging event closes the gap before the last work block. CEV SOE tracks in parallel: A0 at 9.6 → 8.5 → 7.3 → 6.0 kWh; A1S at 7.7 → 6.6 → 5.4 → 4.2 kWh entering 16:45.
3
16:45 — last field-work interval blocked by the SOE floor constraint
The 16:45 work slot demands 5.234 kW × 0.25 h = 1.309 kWh from the CEV.

A0 CEV entering: 6.045 kWh → after work: 4.736 kWh  ✓ (floor 2.96 kWh, headroom +1.78).
A1S CEV entering: 4.157 kWh → after work: 2.848 kWh  ✗ (0.11 kWh below floor).

The solver correctly refuses to schedule work and instead charges the CEV from the MCS. This is the proximate cause of the missed interval — but the real error occurred 1 hour earlier.
⚠
The SOE floor refusal at 16:45 is correct behaviour. The upstream bug is the MPC's conservative charging decision at 15:45. With a shrinking/receding horizon, the optimizer sees only ~5 remaining intervals and underweights the need to top up the CEV before the final work block. The stochastic variants (A2S, A2R) do not fix this — all 5 scenarios share the same deterministic constraint once the 15:45 charge level is committed.
