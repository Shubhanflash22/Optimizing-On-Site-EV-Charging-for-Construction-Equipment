================================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS ACROSS ALL THREE STEPS
================================================================================
1. Dataset (Step 8): 11 source videos → 306,780 total 16-frame clips @25FPS
   (261,281 train / 45,499 val), 5 activity classes, train/val split by video
   groups with no frame leakage between sets.

2. Training (Step 9): R3D-18 (Kinetics-400 pretrained), only layer4+fc unfrozen
   (75.1% trainable params), 20 epochs, Adam @ LR=0.001, mixed precision.
   Training accuracy climbed to 99.45%, but validation accuracy plateaued
   ~89–91% after epoch ~9 — classic overfitting pattern (train loss kept
   dropping to 0.015 while val loss hovered/rose to 0.40-0.48). Best val loss
   was actually at epoch 4 (0.3498, 89.68% acc), suggesting early stopping
   around epoch 4-9 could generalize better than training the full 20 epochs.
   Final model: 89.68% best val accuracy vs paper's 87.6% (on fewer/different
   classes — not a strictly apples-to-apples comparison).

3. Inference/Evaluation (Step 10): Running full post-processed inference on
   val-only frames across all 11 videos gives 87.92% overall accuracy —
   close to the raw classifier's val accuracy, meaning the post-processing
   (idling-physics + majority voting; FSM disabled) is roughly neutral.
   "loading" remains the weakest class everywhere (lowest support, lowest
   precision/recall in both training and inference: 64-74% range), while
   "idling" is consistently the strongest (>91% recall, >97% precision).
   Per-video accuracy ranges widely (79.14% on Day_2 to 100% on TC_00013,
   the latter having very few val frames so it's not very informative).
